Damien and Odette

An installment of Pattern Recognition from Synesthesia Radio Theater.

Bringing you epic dramatic stories in tidy little podcast-sized chunks.

Although the stories and the characters will be different in each installment, they examine the ways in which people try, and sometimes succeed, in finding meaning in everyday life — whether it’s there or not.

So without further ado, we present the first installment of Pattern Recognition.

This is Damien and Odette.
