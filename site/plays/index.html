My plays tend to have small casts (six or fewer characters) and uncomplicated sets, making them accessible to smaller theaters with modest resources.

Welcome to Paradise

Set on the fictional island of St. Sebastian, the play examines what happens when two peoples’ unexpected and unconventional life choices collide with the comfortable assumptions and well-laid plans of others. World Premiere at the Purple Rose Theatre, 2019; over 100 performances across the U.S. and Canada.
