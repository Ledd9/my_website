Pattern Recognition

Three short comedies about people finding meaning, connection, and certainty in places where they may—or may not—actually exist.

Synopsis

Taking its title from the cognitive science term, Pattern Recognition examines the human propensity to find and give meaning to patterns in everyday life — whether they are actually there or not. At an art gallery, in an old French château, and on a peaceful beach at sunset, our characters encounter things they become convinced have profound meaning for their lives. But heeding those messages and turning them into action can lead to some disappointing — and often ridiculous — surprises.

Served as a full meal or à la carte.

Production history

Pattern Recognition was presented in its entirety in reading form in 2017 by Theatre Now in New York City.

The individual play Damien and Odette was presented as a staged reading in 2024 at the Tusten Theatre in Narrowsburg, New York, as part of the Catskills Creative Residency program by the Mid-Hudson Arts Alliance.
