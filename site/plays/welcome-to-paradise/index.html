Welcome to Paradise

World Premiere: Purple Rose Theatre, Chelsea, Michigan, 2019 · Over 100 performances across the U.S. and Canada

Synopsis

Set on the fictional island of St. Sebastian, Welcome to Paradise examines what happens when two peoples’ unexpected and unconventional life choices collide with the comfortable assumptions and well-laid plans of others whose lives they intersect with.

Trailer

Production history

Welcome to Paradise had its World Premiere at the Purple Rose Theatre in Chelsea, Michigan in 2019. To date it has been performed over 100 times, at regional theaters in both the U.S. and Canada.

Welcome to Paradise is now available in printed book form from Theatrical Rights Worldwide.
