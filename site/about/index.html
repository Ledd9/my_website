Who I am

About Julie

I am a playwright living and working in New York City. My plays tend to have small casts (six or fewer characters) and uncomplicated sets, making them accessible to smaller theaters with modest resources. Everyone deserves to see good theatre, even — or especially — those whose local theaters don’t have Broadway-sized budgets.

Past projects

Theatre Now

In 2013 I joined with three other theatre professionals to form Theatre Now, an artists’ service organization that provides intensive, ongoing support and guidance for the creators of new musical theatre. Theatre Now is also the producer of Sound Bites, an annual festival of 10-minute musicals, now in its 12th year.

Artist in Residence

In October 2023 I was invited to be Artist in Residence with the Catskills Creative Residency at the Tusten Theater in Narrowsburg, NY, for a week-long workshop and presentation of works-in-progress. This program was supported by and under the auspices of the Delaware Valley Arts Alliance and the New York State Council on the Arts.

transitstation

In 2022, I collaborated with the principal development team of transitstation, an international exhibition-as-live-art event featuring performance art, music, theatre, poetry, video, installation, fashion, lectures, workshops and interviews.
