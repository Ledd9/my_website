Privacy Policy

Who we are

Our website address is: https://juliemarino.nyc.

What this site collects

This site is a static website. It has no user accounts, no comment forms, and no tracking or analytics of its own. Browsing the site does not require you to provide any personal information.

Mailing list

If you join the mailing list by email, that is an informal person-to-person list conforming to data handling standards. Julie Marino is the data controller; your details are not shared with anyone; you retain full rights, including the right to request your details be removed at any time by emailing julie@juliemarino.nyc.

Embedded content from other websites

Some pages include embedded content (for example, videos hosted on YouTube). Embedded content from other websites behaves in the exact same way as if you had visited the other website. These websites may collect data about you, use cookies, embed additional third-party tracking, and monitor your interaction with that embedded content.

Fonts

This site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests those font files from Google's servers, which receive standard request data such as your IP address. See Google's privacy policy for details.

External links

Links to external sites (such as Theatrical Rights Worldwide, IPEX Artists Agency, and others) are governed by those sites’ own privacy policies.

Questions
