Who I am

About Julie

I am a playwright and storyteller living and working in New York City. Many of these plays and stories explore questions of agency and autonomy, and of what we owe to ourselves and to others.

My plays tend to have small casts (six or fewer) and uncomplicated sets, making them accessible to smaller theaters with modest resources. Everyone deserves to see good theatre, even — or especially — those whose local companies don’t have Broadway-sized budgets. You can find information about my current projects elsewhere on this site.

Further samples of my work may be seen on the New Play Exchange.

Past projects

transitstation

In 2022, I collaborated with the principal development team of transitstation, an international exhibition-as-live-art event featuring performance art, theatre, poetry, video, installation, fashion, lectures, workshops and interviews.

Theatre Now

In 2013 I joined with three other theatre professionals to form Theatre Now, an artists’ service organization that provides intensive, ongoing support and guidance for the creators of new musical theatre. Theatre Now is also the producer of Sound Bites, an annual festival of 10-Minute musicals, now in its 13th year.

Artist in Residence

In October 2023, I was invited to be Artist In Residence with the Catskills Creative Residency at the Tusten Theater in Narrowsburg, New York. This program was supported by and under the auspices of the Delaware Valley Arts Alliance and the New York State Council on the Arts.
